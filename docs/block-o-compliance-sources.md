# Block O — Compliance sources (Two Fires, demo venue)

Per the 31 Aug 2026 Decision Log entry and Build Manual Block O (O2): every
compliance-relevant SOP section below is grounded in a real, current,
searched source — not plausible-sounding unsourced content. This doc is the
citation record; the module content itself (seeded into the DB) states the

State/territory: **Victoria** (confirmed default per Decision Log — no reason
found to differ from venue #1's Melbourne context).

## Food safety supervisor & food handler training — FSANZ Standard 3.2.2A
- Food Standards Australia New Zealand, *Standard 3.2.2A Food Safety
  Management Tools* (in force from Dec 2023): https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-12/Standard%203.2.2A%20Food%20Safety%20Management%20Tools-%20corrected%20271123.pdf
- Vic Health, *Introducing Standard 3.2.2A*: https://www.health.vic.gov.au/food-safety/introducing-standard-322a-food-safety-management-tools
- Applied: every food business serving/selling food needs a certified Food
  Safety Supervisor (FSS certification valid 5 years); food handlers need
  training covering safe handling, contamination, cleaning/sanitising, and
  personal hygiene before handling high-risk food. Two Fires is a Category 1
  business (cooks/handles high-risk food — fried food, dairy, meat) so needs
  all three tools: supervisor, food handler training, records.

## Food temperature control — FSANZ Standard 3.2.2
- FSANZ, *Standard 3.2.2 Food Safety Practices and General Requirements*:
  https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/publications/SiteAssets/Pages/safefoodaustralia3rd16/Standard%203.2.2%20Food%20Safety%20Practices%20and%20General%20Requirements.pdf
- FSANZ, *Keeping food at the right temperature*: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/keeping-food-at-the-right-temperature
- Applied: potentially hazardous food held at 5°C or colder, or 60°C or
  hotter. Cooling: 60°C→21°C within 2 hours, then 21°C→5°C within a further
  4 hours. Used in Kitchen Opening & Closing (walk-in/fridge checks) and
  Food Safety & Hygiene modules.

## Allergen declarations — FSANZ Standard 1.2.3 / PEAL
- FSANZ, *Allergen labelling for food businesses*: https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/labelling/allergen-labelling
- Mandatory declarable allergens under Standard 1.2.3/Schedule 9: cereals
  containing gluten, crustacean, egg, fish, milk, tree nuts, peanuts,
  sesame, soybeans, lupin, added sulphites (≥10mg/kg), royal jelly.
  Plain English Allergen Labelling (PEAL) transition period concluded
  25 Feb 2026 — mandatory specified terms now required. Applied in
  Allergen Handling module, including shared-fryer-oil cross-contact risk
  specific to Two Fires' fried menu.

## Victorian RSA (Responsible Service of Alcohol)
- Liquor Control Victoria / VCGLR-approved RSA training requirement:
  https://blog.foodsafety.com.au/how-to-get-a-responsible-service-of-alcohol-certificate-in-victoria
- Applied: anyone selling/supplying/serving alcohol at a licensed Victorian
  premises must hold an RSA certificate from a Liquor Control Victoria
  approved provider; 28 days from start of role to complete; certificate
  valid 3 years. Used in Bar Service & RSA Compliance module.

## Liquor Control Reform Act 1998 (Vic) — intoxication & minors
- Austlii, *Liquor Control Reform Act 1998 (Vic) s119 (supplying liquor to
  minors)*: https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/lcra1998266/s119.html
- Better Health Channel, *Alcohol and the law*: https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/health/healthyliving/alcohol-and-the-law
- Applied: licensees/staff must not serve a person who is intoxicated
  (speech/balance/coordination/behaviour noticeably affected); intoxicated
  patrons may stay but not be served further; must not serve/supply anyone
  under 18 (licensee penalty 120 penalty units, employee penalty 20 penalty
  units). Used in Bar Service & RSA Compliance module.

## Deep fryer fire safety — Class F extinguishers, AS 2444
- Fire Extinguisher Online, *Wet Chemical Fire Extinguisher: Australian
  guide*: https://www.fireextinguisheronline.com.au/blog/post/wet-chemical-fire-extinguisher-australian-guide
- Applied: cooking oil/fat fires are Class F; a wet chemical (Class F,
  typically 7L/3F rating) extinguisher is mandatory near deep fryers under
  AS 2444; water must never be used on a hot-oil fire (flash-boils to steam,
  atomises burning oil into a fireball). Used in Deep Fryer Safety module
  and cross-referenced in Emergencies & Incidents.

## Beer line cleaning & cellar hygiene
- Cellar Maintenance Australia, *Beer Line Cleaning & Draught System
  Maintenance Guide*: https://cellarmaintenance.com.au/beer-maintenance-guide/
- Applied: Australian industry standard is fortnightly full beer line
  cleaning with a food-grade line-cleaning chemical (weekly for
  high-volume venues); daily tap/nozzle rinsing; gas system checks to
  AS 5034. Used in Beer Line & Cellar Hygiene module.

## Pizza oven / grill heat safety
- General commercial-kitchen burn-prevention guidance (contact surfaces on
  a pizza deck oven run hotter than a standard oven; fire suppression over
  cooking equipment is standard/required in commercial kitchens).
- Applied: PPE (dry, not damp, oven cloths — damp cloths flash to steam on
  contact), never touching the deck/interior when hot, keeping the pass
  clear during pizza service. Used in Pizza Oven & Grill Safety module.
