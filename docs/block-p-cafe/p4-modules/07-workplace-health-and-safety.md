# Workplace health and safety

**Applies to:** Owner/Manager, Cook, Senior Barista, Barista/FOH, Kitchen Hand/FOH Casual

---

## Section 1: Manual handling

Milk crate deliveries come in heavy, twice a week, and outdoor furniture gets moved in and out depending on the weather. Lift with your knees, not your back, and ask for help with anything heavy or awkward rather than pushing through alone.

- B. Lift with your knees and ask for help rather than working alone (correct)
- C. Only lift it if you've done it before
- D. There's no guidance, use your own judgement

## Section 2: Chemical safety

Glass washer and dishwasher chemicals are stored under the sink in the kitchen, in their original, labelled containers. Never transfer a chemical into an unlabelled bottle or a container that used to hold something else.

> **Safety critical: cleaning chemicals stay in their original, labelled containers, always.** Never decant into an unlabelled bottle.

## Section 3: Evacuation and occupancy

Our seating is generally described as 42 seats inside and 8 on the footpath, but that number has been questioned internally, with a more recent estimate closer to 35 for how the space actually operates day to day. Neither number has been formally confirmed against an official source. For a genuine evacuation or fire safety situation, don't rely on either figure from memory, check with Renata for the venue's actual, confirmed occupancy figure rather than assuming the seating count is the same thing as a safe maximum occupancy number.

## Section 4: Reporting hazards

If you spot a hazard or have a near miss, tell Renata directly. There's no written form for this at The Batch House right now, it's a verbal report, so it only works if people actually speak up rather than assuming someone else will mention it.
