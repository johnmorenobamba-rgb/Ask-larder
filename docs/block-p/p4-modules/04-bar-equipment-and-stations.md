# Bar equipment and stations

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor, Bar Attendant, Glassie

---

## Section 1: Beer lines and taps

Our beer lines run through a Lancer Taphouse system behind the main bar, with four taps pouring straight from kegs kept in the cool room. Pour beer at a slight angle down the side of the glass to control the head, then straighten up as the glass fills.

The lines get cleaned on a fortnightly rotation. If a beer starts pouring cloudy, foamy, or with an off taste that a fresh keg doesn't explain, tell Priya or Jordan rather than assuming it's the keg.

## Section 2: The glasswasher

Our glasswasher is a Hobart PROFI FX unit fitted under the bar counter. It runs a full wash and rinse cycle in a couple of minutes, using detergent and a rinse aid dosed automatically from their containers under the back bar.

Load glasses upside down and give the machine a moment to finish its cycle before opening it. Running low on rinse aid leaves glasses spotty, so flag it early rather than after a service starts.

## Section 3: The espresso machine

We run a compact Wega Mininova machine, used only for espresso martinis rather than a coffee program, so you won't be pulling shots for guests to drink on their own. Pull a fresh shot for each espresso martini ordered rather than batching shots ahead of time, since the drink relies on that shot being hot and freshly extracted.

Wipe down the group head and steam wand after each use, and flag any grinder issue to Priya or Jordan rather than working around it.

## Section 4: Cool room and kegerator

Our single walk in cool room is Bromic branded and holds kegs, wine, and board ingredients together. It's a shared space, so keep food sealed and away from anything that could drip onto it, and keep kegs clear of the walkway.

The kegerator sits under the bar and runs two extra lines beyond the main four tap system, giving us six pouring lines in total when everything's connected.

## Section 5: POS and EFTPOS terminals

We run three Square terminals: the main bar terminal, a second till, and a roaming Square Stand used in the function nook. The second till only comes online on Friday and Saturday nights when we're busy enough to need it.

Log every sale as it happens rather than running a tab in your head, this keeps our till counts accurate at the end of the night and makes reconciliation faster for whoever's closing.

## Section 6: Looking after the gear

Report anything that looks, sounds, or smells wrong with any piece of equipment straight away rather than waiting to see if it resolves itself. A glasswasher running a longer cycle than usual, a tap pouring unevenly, or a fridge that feels warmer than it should are all worth flagging immediately.

Equipment issues caught early are a quick fix. Left until they fail mid service, they become a bigger problem for everyone on shift.
