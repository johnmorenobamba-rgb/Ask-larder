## Section 1: The safe combination

The safe combination is 14-32-08. Marcus resets the safe combination every quarter and gives it to you directly and verbally when you take on the Duty Manager role, and again at every reset.

Never write it down anywhere, including in handover notes, and never pass it to anyone else, including another Duty Manager who hasn't been given it directly by Marcus yet. If you forget it, go straight to Marcus rather than guessing or trying an old reset.
