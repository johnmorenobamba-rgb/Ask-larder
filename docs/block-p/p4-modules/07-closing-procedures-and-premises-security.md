# Closing procedures and premises security

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor

---

## Section 1: Who can close, and why

Only the Duty Manager or the Bar Supervisor closes The Quiet Fox. A Bar Attendant is never left to close alone, even on a quiet Tuesday with barely anyone in the room.

This isn't about trust, it's about making sure whoever locks up is authorised to make the judgement calls that come with closing: reconciling cash, securing stock, and checking the premises is actually clear before the alarm's set.

## Section 2: The closing sequence

Run this sequence every night, in this order, no matter how quiet the shift was.

1. Call last drinks 30 minutes before the licensed close time for that night. No new drinks are served after the close time itself.
2. Reconcile both tills in use that night and run the Square Z read on each.
3. Lock up stock: the spirits cage, the cool room, and disconnect any keg that's due to be swapped before the next shift.
4. Do a quick CCTV review of the night, checking specifically for anything that was flagged verbally during service.
5. Set the alarm.
6. Do a final walk through, turn the lights off, and lock the front and back doors.

## Section 3: Trading hours and the outdoor conditions

Nothing at The Quiet Fox trades past 1am on any night, since we don't hold a late night endorsement. Under Victorian licensing rules, once trading hours actually end, guests already holding a drink get a short window to finish it, but nothing new can be poured, which is exactly why we call last drinks 30 minutes early rather than right on close.

The footpath trading strip has its own conditions: it closes at 10pm, and there's no amplified music outdoors after 8pm, to manage noise for the apartments above the shops next door. Both are easy to forget on a warm Thursday when the crowd wants to spill outside, so build them into how you read the room from mid evening, not just at close.

> **Safety critical: nothing trades past 1am, the footpath closes at 10pm, and no amplified music plays outdoors after 8pm.** These are licence conditions, not house preferences.

## Section 4: CCTV review at close

The six cameras cover the entry, main bar, back bar, function nook, back door, and footpath strip, with footage retained for 30 days. Every closing night, do a quick review focused on anything that was flagged verbally during the shift, rather than watching the whole night back.

If an actual incident occurred, that review needs to be more thorough, and it's worth doing while the details are still fresh rather than leaving it for the next day.

## Section 5: Banned patrons at close

We keep a folder behind the bar with a photo and a short note on anyone who's been asked not to return. It's a physical folder, not a digital list, so check it as part of your closing routine if anything unusual happened that night involving a patron who might need to be added.

If you do add someone, note it clearly enough that whoever's on the door next time can recognise them from the description alone.
