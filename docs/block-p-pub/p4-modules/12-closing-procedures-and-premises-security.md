# Closing procedures and premises security

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor

---

## Section 1: Till reconciliation and lockup

Till reconciliation and stock lockup are done by the closing duty manager, usually Dave, sometimes Steph. This happens every closing shift, regardless of how the night went.

**Check question:** Who's responsible for till reconciliation and stock lockup at close?
- A. Whoever's still on shift at close
- B. The closing duty manager, usually Dave or Steph (correct)
- C. Kitchen staff before they leave
- D. It rotates weekly

---

## Section 2: Last drinks

Last drinks are called at about 12:30, for a 1am close. It can shift a little depending on how busy the night is, but 12:30 is the timing to work from.

**Check question:** Roughly when are last drinks called for a 1am close?
- A. Midnight
- B. About 12:30 (correct)
- C. 12:55
- D. There's no set timing

---

## Section 3: CCTV

CCTV is reviewed reactively rather than watched live every night. If something's happened, footage gets pulled and reviewed, it isn't monitored as a standing task through service.

**Check question:** When is CCTV footage reviewed at The Coachman's Arms?
- A. Live, every night, by whoever's on the floor
- B. Reactively, if something's happened (correct)
- C. Only once a month
- D. It isn't reviewed at all

---

## Section 4: Restricted access at close

The safe code and the alarm code are both restricted to Gary, Dave, and Steph. If you're one of the three and closing that night, the specifics of accessing each are covered in your own restricted training, not in this module.

**Check question:** Who has access to the safe code and alarm code at The Coachman's Arms?
- A. Every Duty Manager and Bar Supervisor
- B. Gary, Dave, and Steph (correct)
- C. Whoever is closing that night
- D. All bar staff
