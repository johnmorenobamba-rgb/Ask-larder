# Business continuity and emergencies

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor, Bartender, Floor Staff, Cellarman, Head Chef, Sous Chef, Kitchen Hand

---

## Section 1: Emergency contacts

We keep emergency contacts on file for an electrician and our payment system provider. If the payment system stops working mid service, tell Dave immediately so he can start sorting it out, rather than trying to troubleshoot it yourself.

- A. Keep trying different cards until one works
- B. Tell Dave immediately (correct)
- C. Wait until the end of the shift to mention it
- D. Turn the system off and on yourself

---

## Section 2: Running low on stock on a big night

If we're about to run out of a keg on a big night, the practical fallback has been to ring the pub up the road and borrow one, paying it back on the next delivery. It's an informal arrangement rather than a standing contract, worked out case by case when it's actually needed.

Whoever's Duty Manager on shift makes this call, not something a bar attendant or floor staff member should organise on their own.

**Check question:** What's the current fallback if a keg runs out on a big night?
- A. Close that tap for the rest of the night, no exceptions
- B. The Duty Manager arranges to borrow one from the pub up the road (correct)
- C. Water down the remaining kegs
- D. There's no fallback, it's whatever happens

## Section 3: Insurance and reporting

Insurance broker details are held by Dave and by Gary's accountant, not something floor staff need to carry day to day. If something happens that might need reporting for insurance purposes, an incident, damage, anything like that, tell Dave or Gary and let them handle the paperwork and the broker contact directly.

- B. Tell Dave or Gary and let them handle it (correct)
- C. Wait to see if it comes up later
- D. Note it in the cleaning schedule
