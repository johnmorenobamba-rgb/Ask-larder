# Crowd control and door security

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor, Bartender, Floor Staff

---

## Section 1: When crowd controllers work

Crowd controllers work Friday and Saturday nights, and Thursday nights when there's a band on. Numbers vary with how big the night is, usually two, sometimes three.

They're contracted in for those shifts rather than being part of our regular staff, so you won't see the same faces every week.

**Check question:** On which nights does The Coachman's Arms usually run crowd controllers?
- A. Every night we're open
- B. Friday and Saturday, and Thursday if there's a band on (correct)
- C. Sunday only
- D. We don't use crowd controllers

---

## Section 2: ID scanning

ID scanning at the door runs Friday and Saturday nights only. Outside those nights, staff still check ID the normal way behind the bar, scanning itself just isn't running.

**Check question:** On which nights does ID scanning run at the door?
- A. Every night
- B. Friday and Saturday only (correct)
- C. Only when a crowd controller happens to bring the scanner
- D. We don't scan ID at the door

---

## Section 3: Bag checks

Bag checks happen at the door on busier nights, when crowd controllers are working, rather than as a standing rule every night. If you're on the door on a quieter night, this isn't something you need to run yourself.

**Check question:** When do bag checks happen at The Coachman's Arms?
- A. Every night, as a standing policy
- B. On busier nights, when crowd controllers are on (correct)
- C. Only if a patron looks suspicious
- D. Never, we don't check bags

---

## Section 4: Staying in touch with the door

Right now, crowd controllers carry their own radios and bar staff don't share a channel with them. If you need to reach the door during a busy shift, you currently have to get someone's attention across the room rather than radio through.

This is a real gap in how we communicate, worth being aware of rather than assuming there's a faster way to reach the door than there actually is. If something urgent is happening near the door, send someone over in person rather than waiting for the right moment to shout across the room.

- A. A shared radio channel
- B. By getting their attention across the room, there's no shared channel yet (correct)
- C. Text message
- D. Through the till system
