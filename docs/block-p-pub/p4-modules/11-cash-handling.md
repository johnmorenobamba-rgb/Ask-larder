# Cash handling

**Applies to:** Duty Manager, Bar Supervisor

---

## Section 1: Float and tills

Our float is $500, split across the two tills. Count your till against this figure at the start of your shift, and flag any discrepancy to whoever's Duty Manager straight away rather than at the end of the night.

**Check question:** What's our standard float across the two tills?
- A. $200
- B. $500 (correct)
- C. $1,000
- D. There's no fixed float

---

## Section 2: Safe drops

Safe drops happen roughly hourly on a busy night, done by whoever's the duty manager on shift. This keeps the amount of cash sitting in the till manageable rather than building up across a long service.

**Check question:** How often do safe drops happen on a busy night?
- A. Once, at the end of the night
- B. Roughly hourly (correct)
- C. Only if a till starts overflowing
- D. Twice a week

---

## Section 3: EFTPOS reconciliation

EFTPOS is reconciled nightly against the till by the closing manager. This should happen every night we trade, not just on nights that feel busy or unusual.

**Check question:** Who reconciles EFTPOS against the till each night?
- A. Whoever opened that morning
- B. The closing manager (correct)
- C. Gary, the next day
- D. It isn't reconciled nightly

---

## Section 4: Petty cash

Anything under $50 doesn't need sign off. Anything above that needs Dave or Gary to sign off. Petty cash itself is kept in a small lock box in the office.

**Check question:** Who needs to sign off on a petty cash withdrawal above $50?
- A. Any Duty Manager
- B. Dave or Gary (correct)
- C. Steph
- D. No sign off is needed at any amount
