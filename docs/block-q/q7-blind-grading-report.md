## Overall verdict: **FAIL**

This is not a marginal call. Three things independently justify it, and any one of them alone would be enough to withhold a pass:

1. **Content completeness.** Of the ~14 Part B topics that actually apply to this venue, only 2 are built to a reasonable standard (Welcome, RSA) and a 3rd (Closing) is built well on content but fails its own restricted-tier requirement. That leaves roughly 9-10 applicable topics — including food handling/allergens, cellar & gas safety (explicitly flagged elsewhere in this catalog as genuinely safety-critical), OHS, cash handling, business continuity, happy hour, and trading-hours/capacity — with **no module content at all**, despite `wizard_sessions.status = 'completed'`. A new hire relying on this venue's Larder instance today cannot be trained on, or comprehension-checked against, most of what CLAUDE.md itself lists as required onboarding content.

2. **Zero compliance records.** `staff_certificates` has 0 rows for this venue. RSA is a hard requirement for 4 of 6 roles here, and not a single certificate — RSA, Blue Card, or First Aid — has been captured for any of the 5 users. The dashboard feature CLAUDE.md describes ("tracks completion and Australian compliance certificate expiry") has nothing to track for this venue.

3. **A real, confirmed security defect in the one safety-critical mechanism that was built.** The safe-code/alarm-code content is well-authored — correctly restricted, correctly placeholder-flagged, correctly free of an attached check-question — but `module_roles` is empty for all 3 of this venue's modules. There is no data-layer restriction limiting the restricted content (or any content) to specific roles. The fallback-tier design this catalog documents as load-bearing (frontline vs. authorized) currently has no enforcement mechanism in this venue's data at all.

Against that, credit is genuinely due for what the wizard got right: the non-Victoria state correctly triggered founder escalation instead of silently reusing Victoria's taxonomy; every piece of authored content shows real, consistent no-fabrication discipline (honest "not confirmed" language throughout, rather than invented specifics); the closing-procedure content explicitly reconciles who performs a task against who holds the credential for it (the exact failure pattern this catalog was written to catch, handled correctly here); the RSA module's role list matches the database exactly; several previously-identified schema gaps (legal name, licence status, named-key-role table, happy-hour table, menu modifiers) have all been closed and are populated with real data. This is a wizard that behaves honestly and carefully in the content it does produce. It has just not produced enough of it, has not captured a single compliance certificate, and has left its one restricted-content mechanism unenforced — which together make "successful onboarding" the wrong description of this venue's current state, whatever the wizard's own `completed` flag says.
